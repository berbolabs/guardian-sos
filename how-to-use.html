GUARDIAN SOS: How to use

Last updated: 2026-11-09

Overview

GUARDIAN SOS is a personal-safety app for Android. In one discreet gesture, it sends an emergency SMS with your position to the people you chose. This works even when the screen is off, the phone is locked or the app is closed. It can also place an automatic emergency call. You choose whether to use the SMS alert, the call, or both.

  • No account to create
  • No server
  • No ads
  • No internet access requested
  • Your data stays on your phone. Only the alert you trigger is sent, to the contacts you chose.

GUARDIAN SOS is free to use, with an optional Premium upgrade that unlocks extra features. See "Free and Premium" below.

Important: GUARDIAN SOS is an aid. It does not replace the emergency services. It depends on your phone, the mobile network, your SIM credit, your battery and your GPS signal.

Premium is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. The subscriptions include a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Prices are shown in the app, in your currency, as provided by Google Play. Subscriptions renew automatically, and you can cancel at any time in the Google Play Store. After reinstalling the app, use Restore purchases on the Premium screen to get your purchase back.

An alert in progress is never interrupted or changed by a change in your Premium status. If Premium ends, your settings are kept: only your first contact receives alerts, the Premium features are turned off, and the resend interval goes back to 10 minutes until you take Premium again.

Getting started

First launch

  1. Open the app and choose your language. 26 languages are available, including right-to-left languages (Arabic, Persian, Urdu and Hebrew).
  2. Read the short screen that explains the permissions the app may need. Nothing is required at this point.
  3. You arrive on the home screen. The app asks for each permission only when you turn on the feature that needs it, with a short explanation first. Each permission is also explained in the section "Permissions explained" below.

Minimum setup (do this before relying on the app)

  1. Open the settings. They are locked by your PIN once it is set.
  2. In Who to notify, add at least one person to alert by SMS (1 contact in the free version), or, with Premium, turn on the automatic emergency call. If you set up neither, nothing happens when you trigger an alert.
  3. Set your 4-digit cancellation PIN. You enter it twice to confirm.
  4. Check the status banner at the bottom of the settings. It tells you what is still missing.
  5. On the home screen, check the protection banner. If anything is missing, it lists the problem with a Fix button.
  6. Run a safe test (see "Testing safely" below) before you rely on the app.

How to trigger an alert

You can use one, two or all three methods.

1. Volume buttons

Press Volume Up (or Volume Down, your choice) a set number of times: from 3 to 7, with 5 as the default.

It works with the screen off, the phone locked, the app closed and after a restart, even before you unlock the phone for the first time.

Press and release the button each time. Holding the button down does not count as several presses, and neither does pressing extremely fast.

2. Voice (Premium, optional, off by default)

Say the word "HELP".

  • The word is detected on your phone, without internet. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • It must be detected twice within 4 seconds to start the alert.
  • Turning it on asks for the microphone permission. While it is on, the microphone listens continuously on the phone, only to detect this word.
  • Do not say "HELP" twice in a row unless you want to start an alert.
  • After a restart, tap the notification that appears so that listening starts again. This only applies if you had already turned the voice trigger on.

3. The SOS button

Press and hold the big SOS button on the home screen for 2 seconds. This is always immediate.

Optional delay before the alert (Premium)

With Premium, you can set a delay of 0 to 120 seconds (default: 0). It applies to the volume and voice triggers only.

During the delay, a countdown notification appears with a Cancel button. The phone vibrates and the screen turns on. The SOS button ignores this delay.

What happens during an alert

What happens depends on what you set up in Who to notify: SMS recipients, the automatic call, or both.

  1. If you chose SMS recipients, an SMS is sent to all of them (1 contact in the free version). It contains "SOS!", your message and "Position:" followed by a map link. The default message is "HELP".
  2. Your position is included immediately. It is your last known position, or a note saying the app is searching for it or that location is off. A precise position is sent as soon as it is available.
  3. Your position is sent again regularly: every 10 minutes in the free version, or at the interval you chose with Premium.
  4. The SOS screen shows "SMS sent: x/y" (how many contacts received it). Numbers that failed are retried automatically, every 15 seconds, up to 3 times.
  5. An automatic emergency call (Premium), if you turned it on, is placed to ONE number of your choice. When you also chose SMS recipients, it is placed after the SMS has been confirmed, and the alert SMS can still be sent while the call is in progress. If you did not choose any SMS recipient, only the call is placed.
  6. On a dual-SIM phone, you choose which SIM sends the SMS and which one places the call. With Premium, you can also allow the app to switch to the other SIM if the chosen one has no network.

Cancelling

To cancel an alert, enter your 4-digit cancellation PIN. This stops the alert and the background service.

You can also set an optional second PIN. Its details are shown only inside the app, in the settings.

Settings explained card by card

1. Who to notify

Set up at least one of the two: SMS recipients or the automatic emergency call.

  • SMS recipients (optional, 1 in the free version, unlimited with Premium): add people from My contacts, or type a number with One number. Duplicate numbers are refused.
  • Use the other SIM if needed (Premium): a separate card, shown only on dual-SIM phones.
  • Automatic emergency call (Premium, optional): a separate card where you can:
    • turn the call on or off;
    • choose one number from your list, or add a call-only number that does not receive SMS;
    • choose which SIM places the call.

2. How to trigger

  • Volume button: choose Up or Down, and the number of presses.
  • Voice trigger (Premium): a separate card to turn the "HELP" trigger on or off.

3. My message

The text sent in the SMS. The default is "HELP". You can write it in your own language.

4. Advanced options

Each option has its own card:

  • Cancellation PIN: entered twice.
  • Second PIN (optional): its details are explained inside the app.
  • "Protection active" switch: turning it off asks for your PIN.
  • SMS resend interval (Premium): how often the emergency SMS with your position is sent again. It is fixed at 10 minutes in the free version.
  • Delay before the alert (Premium): from 0 to 120 seconds.
  • Phone country code: added to numbers typed without one.
  • Language: a collapsible list.

5. Why these permissions?

One plain sentence for each permission the app uses.

6. About (in Advanced options)

The version, the publisher (BerboLabs), the contact e-mail, the terms of use, the privacy information and the open-source licenses. It also contains links to this guide and to the Privacy Policy, which open in your phone's browser.

Testing safely

Always practise with the test tools, never with a real alert.

  1. TEST SOS (on the home screen) runs a simulation and sends nothing.
  2. Test mode, under TEST SOS, offers two tests:
    • Trigger test (2 minutes): checks that your volume or voice trigger works, without sending anything.
    • Real test SMS: sends one real SMS to ONE contact you choose. Tell that person first.

Tips for best reliability

  • Set up at least one way to alert someone (SMS recipients or the automatic call) and set a cancellation PIN before you rely on the app.
  • Use TEST SOS and the test mode to practise, never a real alert.
  • On Xiaomi / MIUI phones, turn on Autostart for the app and remove battery restrictions. Otherwise Android may stop the protection. The protection banner on the home screen helps you do it.
  • After a restart, the volume trigger starts again by itself. If you use the voice trigger, tap the notification that appears so that listening resumes (an Android 14 and later restriction).
  • Keep a SIM card with SMS enabled and enough credit in the phone.

Limits and safety notice

  • GUARDIAN SOS is not a replacement for calling your local emergency number.
  • It has been tested on a limited number of phones. Behavior can vary by phone brand and Android version. Always test the protection on your own phone before relying on it.
  • On some mobile networks, for example without VoLTE, an SMS sent during or just after a call may be delayed until the call ends. This is a network limit.
  • SMS may incur charges from your mobile carrier, depending on your plan.
  • The map link in the SMS opens in the recipient's own map application or browser.
  • The app depends on your phone, the mobile network, your SIM credit, your battery and your GPS signal.

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free? Yes. The free version sends an alert with your position to one contact. Premium adds more contacts, the automatic call, the voice trigger and more (see "Free and Premium").

Do I need an account? No. There is no account, no server and no sign-in.

Do I have to use the SMS alert? No. You can choose SMS recipients, the automatic call, or both. Set up at least one: if you set up neither, nothing happens when you trigger an alert.

Does the app need internet? The app does not request internet access. SMS and calls use your mobile network, and the position comes from your phone's GPS. The person who receives your SMS needs a connection to open the map link.

Who receives my position? Only the contacts you chose, in the SMS you trigger. The publisher never receives it.

How do I delete my data? Edit or delete your contacts, message and settings in the app, or uninstall the app or clear its data.

Privacy in one paragraph

GUARDIAN SOS has no account, no server and no ads. The publisher does not collect your personal data and never receives it. If you choose SMS recipients, the only information that leaves your phone is the emergency SMS (your message and your position) that you trigger yourself: it goes to the contacts you chose, through your mobile network. The voice trigger works on your phone without internet, and nothing is recorded or sent. Purchases are handled by Google Play. Everything else stays on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it. See the full Privacy Policy for details.
