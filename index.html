GUARDIAN SOS

GUARDIAN SOS is a personal-safety app for Android. In one discreet gesture, it sends an emergency SMS with your position to the people you chose, even when the screen is off or the phone is locked. It can also place an automatic emergency call.

  • No account, no server, no ads
  • Your data stays on your phone. Only the alert you trigger is sent, to the contacts you chose.

Free to use, with an optional Premium upgrade (more contacts, automatic call, voice trigger and more).

Important: GUARDIAN SOS is an aid. It does not replace the emergency services.
